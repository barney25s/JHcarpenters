Our story
A Lebanese family business, founded in 1955.

Woodworking has been the family’s work for generations, and with it a way of doing things: understand the space, choose the material with care, build it properly.

Today we continue that tradition from our workshop in Kornayel, Mount Lebanon, making doors, windows, kitchens, furniture, interior woodwork and pergolas.

Architecture meets craft
Joseph Hachem brings an architectural background to the workshop.

It shapes how we read drawings, resolve details and work with architects and designers, so that what leaves the workshop fits the building it is made for.

How a custom project works

Brief

You tell us what you need, with drawings, photos or just an idea. We visit and measure where needed.

ii

We agree the details, materials and finish with you, and with your architect or designer.

iii

Make

Your pieces are manufactured in our workshop in Kornayel.

iv

Install
