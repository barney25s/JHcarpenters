Our story
Carpenters by Joseph Hachem & Co. is a Lebanese family business, founded in 1955.

Woodworking has been the family’s work for generations, and with it a way of doing things: understand the space, choose the material with care, build it properly. Today we continue that tradition from our workshop in Kornayel, Mount Lebanon.

Joseph Hachem brings an architectural background to the workshop. It shapes how we read drawings, resolve details and work with architects and designers.
